Questions

Can guests book on my site?

Not directly, on purpose. Booking buttons send guests to Booking.com, Hotels.com, Expedia, your phone or your email, so your rooms and prices stay where you already manage them. The site explains how booking and payment work, so guests book with confidence.

Where does the neighbourhood information come from?

Places come from OpenStreetMap and landmarks from Wikipedia, sorted by walking time. You can hide places you don't recommend, star your favourites, add your own tips and add places we missed.

How automatic is the stay story?

Completely: choose a guest profile and the site writes their arrival, days and departure from the places around you, for 1 to 4 nights, in any season. You can add a tip, recommend rooms or leave things out.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Download the site and host it at your domain, or forward your domain to your link here.

What does it cost?

Nothing while ArviStay is in beta.

Who owns the photos and texts?

You do. You can delete them, or the whole site, at any time. The Privacy & Terms page has the details.

Does it work on phones?

Every hotel site is designed for phones first. The editor works on a computer or a tablet, and in a pinch on a phone.
